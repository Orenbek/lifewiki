# Wiki Governance

## Scope

Use `wikis/` for the author's durable thoughts, knowledge, experiences, questions, and evolving judgments. Default to helping the future author understand and revisit them.

Include:

- Ideas and observations, including brief or unresolved material explicitly offered for capture.
- Personal experiences and judgments, with enough context to preserve their meaning.
- Knowledge and reading notes with attributable sources and the author's interpretation distinguished.
- Topic syntheses, useful disagreements, and meaningful changes in understanding.
- Stable conventions needed to maintain this Wiki.

Exclude:

- Temporary execution logs, one-off status updates, and whole chat transcripts used in place of edited knowledge. Selected author passages may be retained as source material.
- Uncontextualized copies of external material with no source or reason for retention.
- Claims without traceable support when they are presented as established fact.
- Unadopted future structures, processes, or governance mechanisms when they are presented as current practice.

## Authority And Content State

Keep a clear primary page for each bounded topic where one exists. Authority means editorial ownership of that scope, not certainty or a final answer. Preserve different perspectives, situations, and periods when they carry distinct meaning.

Distinguish sourced knowledge, personal observation, adopted judgment, tentative interpretation, and open questions where the difference affects understanding. Do not impose a status field or label on every paragraph.

Clearly identify proposals, inferences, open questions, factual uncertainty, and historical or superseded content in the relevant document body, with enough context for readers to understand its state. Link superseded content to its successor when the history remains useful.

## Governance Layers

This skill owns reusable Wiki behavior, including directory, frontmatter, authoring, retrieval, and maintenance protocol.

Treat `wikis/_meta/constitution.md` as the repository's Wiki constitution. It defines stable Wiki purpose, knowledge boundaries, structural principles, and governance boundaries.

Read and follow it before changing the Wiki. Do not modify it during routine authoring or maintenance; change it only when the repository intentionally revises those principles or boundaries.

Keep the actual domain and page inventory in `wikis/README.md` and domain README files.

## Change Boundaries

Make the smallest coherent change. Update directly affected indexes and links, but do not reorganize neighboring domains without a concrete need.

Before a broad move, merge, or deletion, state how existing pages and their authoritative topics will map to the proposed structure.

When maintenance reveals durable Wiki conventions, update the appropriate governance explanation within the requested scope. Do not turn the author's ordinary conversation into published beliefs without alignment.
